Four note types
Ctrl+N cycles the type. It is always visible, because capturing a meeting as a plain note is the mistake worth preventing.
NOTE▸steering call. finance published the codes finally
→ a dated line in each tagged project's ## Log
MEETING▸arch board. bram wants the terraform decision signed off @arch-board
→ a sitting in ## Minutes, plus every action and decision found inside it
ACTION▸draft the rollback plan @acme !fri
→ ## Next actions with an owner and a due date
DECISION▸we're going with terraform, not ARM @acme
→ a dated line in ## Decisions
