Three modes
Vim's bargain: give up typing-by-default and every single letter becomes a command. Esc is always the way back.
NORMAL
Reading and moving. The resting state.
  • j kmove
  • /search
  • e Eedit line · raw source
  • x d stick · due · status
  • uundo the last commit
i o→←Esc
INSERT
Typing. Into the modeline, a line, or the capture pane.
  • ia note in the modeline
  • othe full-screen pane
  • ethe focused line, in place
  • ⏎commit it
  • Escback, keeping the text
:→←Esc
COMMAND
A brain verb, typed out. Completion on Tab.
  • :logagainst the selection
  • :statusset a field
  • :agendabuild one for a board or person
  • :metrecord the meeting
  • :inboxwhat is waiting
THE MODELINE, IN EACH MODE
-- NORMAL --▸acme-migrationNOTE3 unprocessed · ? help
-- INSERT --NOTE▸bram wants the terraform decision signed off⏎ capture · esc
-- COMMAND --:agenda arch-board⏎ run · esc cancel
The bottom line is the whole state of the app: which mode you are in, what is selected, what i would capture as, and how much is waiting to be processed. Nothing else on screen changes between modes, so you never lose your place.
